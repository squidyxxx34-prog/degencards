FAQ

What is DEGENCARDS?

A web app for degen traders: every crypto trade becomes a collectible card with rarity, grade and achievements, plus a PnL card image and a trade replay video you can share on X, TikTok, Instagram or Telegram.

How do I make a PnL card for a Solana trade?

Paste your Solana wallet address on DEGENCARDS. Your closed trades are imported automatically and each one becomes a PnL card with the real chart, entry, exit, hold time and profit. Tap Share to download it as an image or a video.

Does it work with Pump.fun, Axiom, Photon and Phantom?

Yes. Any Solana wallet works: Pump.fun, Axiom, Photon, BullX, GMGN, Fomo, Phantom, Solflare or Backpack. Use the address of the wallet that actually makes the trades.

Is it safe to paste my wallet address?

Yes. DEGENCARDS only reads public on-chain data from your public address. It never asks for a signature, a seed phrase or a private key, and cannot move your funds.

Is it free?

Yes. Auto-import, PnL cards, animated cards and trade replays are free. PRO adds replay customization, advanced stats and more card styles.

What is a trade replay?

A short vertical video that replays your trade candle by candle on the real chart, with your buys and sells and your live profit, then shows the final result. Made to be posted on TikTok, Reels or X.

Can I show a losing trade?

Yes. Every closed trade becomes a card, wins and losses alike, with its own rarity from common to mythic.
