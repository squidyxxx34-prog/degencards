FAQ

What is DEGENCARDS?

A web app that turns every crypto trade into a collectible card, a PnL card image and a trade replay video you can share. Made for the degen crypto community.

Does it work with Pump.fun and Fomo?

Yes. Pump.fun and Fomo trades are on-chain: connect the public address of your wallet and DEGENCARDS reads your buys and sells, including pump.fun cashback and real fees.

Can it touch my funds?

No. It only reads a public key. No wallet connection, no signature, nothing can be moved.

Is it free?

Cards, auto-import and sharing are free. PRO unlocks replay customization and full stats.

DEGENCARDS en français ?

Oui : DEGENCARDS transforme chaque trade de degen en carte à collectionner, carte PnL et vidéo de trade replay. Import automatique depuis un wallet Solana (Pump.fun, Fomo, Phantom), en lecture seule.
